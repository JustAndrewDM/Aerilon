## Calendar and Timekeeping
The world of Aerilon runs on a calendar similar to feudal China, where distinct eras are marked by the Primarch God, or the god who holds the most devotion. These years are marked by the First letter within the God's name, i.e. E487 for the 487th year of [[Eldis, God of Order]]. 
From here, the years are broken into four seasons consisting of 80 days each. Sylvareth (Syl) which is skin to Spring, Vuldrath (Vul) which is akin to Summer, Ruswyn (Rus) which is akin to Fall, and Frosthald (Fro) which is akin to Winter.
A date is written as follows E487 - Syl 58.
