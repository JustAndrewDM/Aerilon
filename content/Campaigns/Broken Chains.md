>**The Fractured Balance**
>_In the world of **Aerilon**, the balance of divine power is maintained through a natural cycle where gods rise and fall as the devotion of mortals shifts from one to another. Each era is marked by the rule of a single god, ensuring no one deity dominates for too long, preserving the essence of humanity. However, this delicate balance has been disrupted._
>
_In the current Era, that of [[Eldis, God of Order]], the rise of [[The Ivory Oath]]—a group of devout protectors—has cemented Eldis’s place as the **Pinnacle God** for far longer than the cycle allows. The Oath, founded by five local heroes and now a thriving organization based in the city of [[Greyross]], has brought stability to [[Teloria]]. However, their collective devotion to Eldis has created an unnatural stagnation in the cycle of power, leading to growing unrest._
>
_Opposing Eldis is [[Thuris, God of Ambition]], who believes that balance has been shattered. Thuris argues that Eldis, through the Ivory Oath, has artificially prolonged his reign and weakened humanity by suppressing ambition and change. To restore the cycle, Thuris has begun gathering those disillusioned with Eldis’s order into a coalition known as  [[The Templars of the Profane]], with the goal of disrupting Eldis’s influence and overthrowing him as Pinnacle God._

This campaign follows the "Evil" group that are trying to prove their devotion to [[Thuris, God of Ambition]] by growing in rank within [[The Templars of the Profane]].
