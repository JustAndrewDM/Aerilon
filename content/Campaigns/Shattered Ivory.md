This campaign is the "good guy" campaign. It follows our group of PCs growing in rank through [[The Ivory Oath]]. The campaign begins when the final High Order of the oath is killed by [[Nazus Malum]]. This order sent a vision to the rest of the the Ivory Oath so they could see that the group had been killed.
