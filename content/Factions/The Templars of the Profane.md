The Templars of the Profane are a growing army who's devotion belongs to [[Thuris, God of Ambition]]. They were founded in E374 as the desire for ambition and change was ever present and growing within the population. This group follows a hierarchal structure, though moving through the ranks is heavily influenced by the ambition of the group that seeks more power.

This group currently resides in [[The Obsidian Crucible]], they home place of Thuris. It is here they forge their artifacts, as well as their destinies. They will use what intelligence they have to seek those who may dissent from the order [[Eldis, God of Order]] tries to provide to the people of Teloria.

Followers of Thuris, they seeks to restore the natural cycle of divine power by undermining the Ivory Oath and turning the people of Teloria against Eldis. They pursue ambitious plans to spread discord, disrupt faith, and prove that stagnation is more dangerous than change.

## Structure
1. [[Thuris, God of Ambition]]
Thuris runs the Templars of the profane, giving them reason to keep fighting and persuing their end goal. He is able to corrupt the power within artifacts given to followers of Eldis and turns them into [[Tools of Ambition]].

4. The Blades of Ambition (The Ashmarked)
To become a blade is to truly become a follower of Thuris. Achieving this feat involves completing the Trial of the Anvil. This is a trial with 3 goals.
	1. One must form or join a kindling (A group of 3-5). This group must prove their tactical prowess by successfully obtaining a symbol of Eldis.
	2. They must then use this symbol in the Obsidian Crucible to create a Tool of Ambition by tapping into Thuris's power of ambition. (At this point, if the symbol is a false one, chains will be burned into the arms of the ones who brought it, forever showing the mistake they made.) This results in a Tool which will be bonded to the user. Should this tool be lost or destroyed, the user's mind will begin to slowly rot until it is found.
	3. To complete the Bonding of the tool, as well as the trial, the group must use these tools to accomplish a great feat set out by Thuris upon completion of the tool. Upon completion, the tool will glow with the power of ambition as it pulls the power from the user.

5. The Emberkind
To become an Emberkind, one must complete the Trial of the Spark. This involves navigating the Labyrinth of Chains to fin one's way into the Obsidian Crucible, as well as competing in a fight at the end of this journey, proving they can push through despite being expended.

6. Snuffed Flames (Outcasts)
These are members who's ambition left them, or those who have lost their drive to achieve greatness. There are cast out from the Crucible and left with searing chain marks all the way from their back to their heels to symbolize them walking away from their path.
